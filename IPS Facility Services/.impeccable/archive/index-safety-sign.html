    The system behind it.

    One idea runs through everything: IPS is the safety standard. Five flat inks, one pictogram, one typeface family and one way of moving, built to work on a van door as well as it does on a phone.

    The mark

    A warning triangle with the figure standing, not slipping. It reads at 16px and from across a car park, and it's ownable in a category full of sparkles and mops.

    The pictogram

    The brand's whole argument in a single drawing.

    Five inks. No tints.

    Borrowed from the signage people already obey. Each colour has one job, and it never gets diluted into gradients.

    Caution Yellow#FFC20E
    Primary field. Owns whole surfaces.
    Sign Ink#111111
    Type, borders, night.
    Sign White#F3F3F1
    Reading ground.
    Safe Green#00843D
    Done, signed off, covered.
    Mandatory Blue#0057A8
    Requirements, clinical.

    Type

    Archivo at its widest for the voice of a sign, plus Barlow Condensed for labels, taken from road-sign lettering. Two families instead of five.

    Motion borrows from the physical sign: heavy, deliberate, then settled.
