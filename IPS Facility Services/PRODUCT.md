# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Single standalone static HTML file (user request). Three.js + GSAP/ScrollTrigger from CDN for 3D assets and scroll animation. Built by Cre8tive Sync as a speculative pitch.

## Users

Primary: commercial buyers of contract cleaning in Perth, WA — office and facility managers, strata and property managers, practice managers at medical centres, school business managers, gym and hospitality operators. They are replacing an unreliable cleaner or onboarding a new site, comparing two or three quotes, and need to trust a crew with after-hours keys and alarm codes.

Secondary (pitch audience): the IPS owners, deciding whether to hire Cre8tive Sync.

Domestic cleaning is a secondary offer, footnoted only.

## Product Purpose

IPS Facility Services is a Western Australian-owned commercial cleaning company operating across Perth since 2007. The site must turn a facility manager into a quote request.

## Positioning

Scale plus systems from a local operator: 180+ commercial premises serviced weekly, 12,000+ cleans a year, a step-by-step task list for every cleaner, an easy client feedback loop, and no lock-in contracts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services (as listed on current site): Corporate Buildings, Medical Facility Cleaning, Education Facilities, Retail & Hospitality, Gyms & Fitness, Strip & Seal, COVID Cleaning, Strata Cleaning, Industrial Cleaning, Builders Cleaning.
Offers: free trial for regular clients, $100 off first invoice, no lock-in contracts, consumables supplied.
Contact details: none published — use clearly labelled placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

Name "IPS Facility Services" (IPS). WA-owned. Existing visuals (blue #083b5c / #0b7fab, Elementor/Astra template, five Google font families) carry no equity and are replaced.

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed claims: since 2007; 180+ premises weekly; 12,000+ services/year; $20M business liability insurance; all cleaners police-cleared; eco-friendly products, industrial-grade equipment; OH&S focus. Client logos shown on site include KILN and The Last Local. 23 project photos exist on their server (hotlink-blocked).
Absent — do not fabricate: named testimonials (existing three are anonymous), case-study figures, ratings, certifications (ISO etc.), phone, email, address, ABN.

## Audit findings (current site, Oct 2026)

- Homepage and contact page are injected with hidden casino/pokies SEO spam (409 "casino" mentions in homepage HTML) — site is compromised.
- Contact page is empty: no phone, email, form or address.
- Hero image is a ChatGPT render; generic stock elsewhere.
- Five Google font families x 18 weights loaded; no type system.
- Testimonials are anonymous; Google review widget broken (raw shortcode visible).
- "Value Cleaners" naming inconsistency; duplicated reasons lists; COVID page still in primary nav.

## Product Principles

1. Trust first: they hold keys to other people's buildings — show systems, checks and accountability.
2. Proof over adjectives: lead with real numbers and the process.
3. One action: get a quote / book the free trial.
4. Local and accountable: Perth, WA-owned, since 2007.
